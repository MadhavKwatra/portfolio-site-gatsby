A travel tracker that maps the countries and Indian states you have visited, on a 3D globe or in custom 2D map styles. Add photos to places, see your travel stats, share Polaroid-style stat cards, and ask Gumi, the built-in AI chatbot. I built it solo in April 2025 for [notJust Hack 2025](https://www.notjusthack.com/app/gumigumi), shipped it to Google Play and the App Store within the month, and it made the final showcase.
