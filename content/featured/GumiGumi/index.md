A travel map for every country and all 36 Indian states and union territories, on a 3D globe or in custom 2D map styles. Add photos and notes to places, see your travel stats, share Polaroid-style cards, and ask Gumi, the built-in AI chatbot. I built it solo in April 2025 for [notJust Hack 2025](https://www.notjusthack.com/app/gumigumi), shipped it to Google Play and the App Store within the month, and it made the final showcase. In 2026 I brought it back: restored 124 users and 357 places from backups into a new database, fixed the photo uploads, and shipped it to Google Play again.
