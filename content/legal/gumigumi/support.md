# GumiGumi — Help and support

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

Something not working, an idea, or a missing place? Write to [madhav.kwat+gumigumi@gmail.com](mailto:madhav.kwat+gumigumi@gmail.com?subject=GumiGumi%20support). I read every email and usually reply within a few days.

To help me fix a problem quickly, please include your phone model, Android version, the app version (Profile › About) and what you did just before it happened. A screenshot helps a lot.

## Common questions

### I can't sign in, or my places don't load.

Please update GumiGumi from Google Play. The app's server moved in late 2026, and older versions can no longer reach it. Your places were kept and come back after the update when you sign in with the same Google account.

### I used to sign in with Apple on an iPhone.

The iPhone app is not available at the moment. Your account and places are kept safely and will be there if the iPhone app comes back. You can also ask for a copy of your places by email, or have them deleted on the [delete-account page](/gumigumi/delete-account/).

### How do I delete my account?

See the [delete-account page](/gumigumi/delete-account/). From version 2.0 you can also do it inside the app.

### Can I get a copy of my data?

From version 2.0: Settings › Your data › Export (CSV or JSON). Before that, email me and I will send it to you.

### Does GumiGumi track my location?

No. GumiGumi never tracks you in the background. A place is saved only when you add it yourself.

### Why does my map show India's borders this way?

Maps in GumiGumi show India's borders as officially recognised in India.

### Why is a state or union territory missing or counted wrong?

GumiGumi counts all 36: 28 states and 8 union territories. If something looks wrong, email me the place name and what you expected.

### Is GumiGumi free?

Yes. There are no ads and no paywall on the basics.
