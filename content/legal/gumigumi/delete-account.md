# Delete your GumiGumi account

_Last updated: 2 November 2026_

You can delete your GumiGumi account and all its data at any time.

## In the app (version 2.0 and later)

1. Open GumiGumi and go to **Profile › Settings**.
2. Tap **Delete account** (in the Account group).
3. Type **DELETE** and confirm.

Tip: tap **Export my places** first if you want a copy (CSV or JSON).

## Older app version, or no access to the app?

Send an email **from the address you used to sign in** to [madhav.kwat+gumigumi@gmail.com](mailto:madhav.kwat+gumigumi@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20GumiGumi%20account&body=Please%20delete%20my%20GumiGumi%20account%20and%20all%20its%20data.%20I%20signed%20in%20with%20this%20email%20address.) with the subject "Delete my GumiGumi account". We delete the account and all its data within 30 days and reply when it is done.

## What gets deleted

- Your account: email, name and profile photo
- All your places, notes and dates
- All photos you uploaded: places, avatar and cover

## What is kept

Nothing linked to your account. Crash reports (no name or email) expire after 30 days. Weekly database backups roll off within 30 days. In app version 1.0.x, Vexo keeps anonymous usage events tied only to a random install ID, not to your account; ask us and we will ask Vexo to delete them.
